Keep as many counters as you need, each with its own name and value.

There are four ways to count:
* tap the + and - buttons
* tap the number, or swipe it up or down
* press the volume keys
* use the home screen widget without opening the app

Long-press the number to reset a counter. You can also turn on sounds and vibration, keep the screen on, hide the buttons, and choose a light or dark theme.

Export your counters in CSV format, or import them from a CSV file.

Counter doesn't collect any data. Everything you create stays on your device.
